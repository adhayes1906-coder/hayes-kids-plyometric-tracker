Training rhythm Warm up, land quietly, hold every stop, sprint relaxed, recover well, and only progress when every rep looks clean.

Training rule: keep the basketball workout and sprinter workout plan separate when possible. Use adult supervision, warm up first, keep jump volume low, avoid all-out indoor sprinting, use only a low sturdy box, and stop for sharp pain, dizziness, unusual symptoms, or form that breaks down.

Rules

  • Use athletic posture and move slowly enough to own each rep.
  • Count clean landings, not max effort jumps. Every jump should finish with a balanced 2-second hold.
  • Do not increase jump work unless balance, stops, and landings are improving too.
  • For box work, jump onto the box, land and stick, then step down. Do not jump down.
  • Every workout includes an upper-body push, pull, or carry using home equipment.
  • Use the Sprinter Plan tab for the separate 200/400m sprinter workout plan.
  • Skip depth jumps, high boxes, and max jump testing unless a qualified coach is supervising.

Coach Notes

simple and repeatable

Weekly Schedule

  • Best rhythm: Monday, Wednesday, Friday.
  • Second option: Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday.
  • Keep one rest day between plyometric sessions.

Progression

  • Weeks 1-2: build jump rhythm, stable landings, and clean upper-body form.
  • Weeks 3-4: add one rep only when jump form, balance, and push/pull form all look clean.
  • Weeks 5-6: add one set to one jump drill, one stability drill, or one upper-body drill, not all on the same day.
  • If jump height improves but landings wobble, repeat the week before progressing.

Stability Booster

  • Use on 1-2 non-workout days if legs feel fresh.
  • Do single-leg balance, heel-to-toe walk, star taps, and quiet line taps.
  • Keep it easy: about 5 minutes, no max jumps.

Sprint Alignment

  • Basketball jumping supports ankle stiffness, hip drive, and elastic takeoff for sprinting.
  • Landing and balance work supports safe deceleration and curve control.
  • Use the separate Sprinter Plan tab for the full 200/400m plan and timeline.

Equipment

  • Low sturdy plyometric box, low step, or bottom stair for box work.
  • Resistance band or mini-band for rows, pull-aparts, presses, and anti-rotation work.
  • Light dumbbells, water jugs, or a backpack for floor press, carries, curls, and rows.
  • Sturdy chair, counter, or wall for incline push-ups and balance support.
  • Tape line, cones or shoes for markers, basketball, mat or towel, water, and open space.
  • Safe flat 10-30 yard outdoor lane only if using sprint build-ups or sprint-float add-ons.
  • Start around 6-12 inches. Choose a height they can land on quietly without knees caving in.

Sprint Rules

  • Sprint work is separate from the basketball workout. Do it on its own day when possible.

  • Fast but relaxed is the goal. Stop when posture, arm action, or landing control breaks down.
